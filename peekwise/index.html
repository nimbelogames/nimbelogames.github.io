How it plays

Two versions of the same picture, one above the other. Tap the places where they differ. Each find is marked on both pictures. Find them all to solve the level.

Questions

Is Peekwise free?

Yes, it's free to play, with ads between levels and optional purchases. A one-time purchase removes the ads between levels. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available.

What are hearts and lives?

Hearts belong to one level: a wrong tap costs a heart. Lives are shared across the game: giving up on a level (out of hearts or out of time) costs a life. Lives refill over time.

How do hints work?

A hint reveals one difference you haven't found. You get hints from Daily Rewards and the prize wheel, or you can use coins or watch an ad.

What is Relaxed mode?

A setting that turns off the timer and gives you unlimited hearts and lives, so levels can't be lost. Look as long as you like.

Who made the pictures?

The scenes, and some of the changes in them, were made with the help of AI. We choose every difference, check each one, and test every level with our own tools.

Something's wrong. Who do I tell?

Write to support@nimbelogames.com, or see the support page.
