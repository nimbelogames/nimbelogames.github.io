Privacy Policy

Nimbelo Games · Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what data our games collect, why, who receives it, and how you can control or delete it. It covers these games on Google Play:

  • Switchlight: Light Puzzle (com.nimbelogames.switchlight)
  • Peekwise: Spot the Difference (com.nimbelogames.peekwise)
  • Lumidart: Logic Puzzle (com.nimbelogames.lumidart)
  • Ridelight: Bus Sort Puzzle (com.nimbelogames.ridelight)

1. Who we are

Nimbelo Games is the developer name of Tal Turgeman, 13 Bilu St., Tel Aviv 6522222, Israel, who is responsible for the data processing described here ("we"). Privacy questions and requests: support@nimbelogames.com

2. The short version

  • We have no player accounts and no servers of our own. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number in the games.
  • Your progress is stored on your device. If you sign in to Google Play Games, a copy is also saved in your Google account (cloud save) so you can continue on another device.
  • The games show ads from Google AdMob. In the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Your privacy choices.
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to count how the levels are played, without your name, email or advertising ID (section 6a).
  • Purchases are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
  • Our games are made for adults and are not directed at children.

3. Data on your device

The games store on your device: your level progress, coins and hints (earned, bought and spent), whether you bought Remove ads, daily rewards and streaks, lives, how many offline levels you have left (and, since you were last offline, whether an ad has shown and how many levels you have played online), the level you are playing, your settings (language, sound, vibration, reduced motion, Relaxed mode), achievement progress, the gift codes redeemed on this phone, and a random install number used to merge cloud saves. This data stays on your device unless you use cloud save (section 4). It is deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game.

4. Google Play Games (sign-in, achievements, cloud save)

Our games don't use Google Play Games yet, so this section doesn't apply for now. Your progress, coins, hints and Remove ads are stored only on your device (section 3).

If you use Google Play Games, the game signs in with your existing Play Games profile. We do not create an account for you.

  • What is sent to Google: your achievements, and a cloud save that contains the progress data listed in section 3 plus a picture from the game, the time played and a short description (for example "Level 42").
  • What we receive: the game can read your Play Games player ID and gamer name/avatar so it can show your profile and keep saves of different accounts apart. We do not store this on any server.
  • Who can read your cloud save: only this game, while you are signed in. It is stored in your Google account under Google's terms.
  • Google processes this data as described in the Google Privacy Policy. By using Play Games, Google's own terms for Play Games Services apply.
  • Play Games is optional: if you do not sign in, the game works and saves only on your device.
  • You can delete your cloud save at any time in the game: Settings > Delete cloud save (shown while cloud save is on, after a confirmation). This deletes the game's save from your Play Games account; your progress on the phone stays, and cloud save stays off on that phone until you turn it on again in Settings > Cloud save. Another phone signed in to the same account can save again. Deleting needs an internet connection.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

The games show ads between levels (not in the first levels) and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a reward. The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's IP address (which can be used to estimate your general location),
  • interactions such as app launches, taps and ad views,
  • diagnostic data such as app launch time and performance,
  • device identifiers: the advertising ID and app set ID.

Google uses this to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. In the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland we show Google's consent form (a Google-certified consent tool) before ads are personalized; if you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalized or limited ads, which use less data. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Your privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. More: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Google can pass ad requests to other advertising companies it works with (ad technology partners), which show and measure ads and receive the data above for that. In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland they are named in the consent message, and you can see and change your choice in Settings > Your privacy choices. Google's list of these partners: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903. Buying Remove ads turns off the ads between levels; optional rewarded ads stay available.

6. Purchases (Google Play)

Coin packs, bundles and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment and sends the game a confirmation of what you bought. We do not receive or store your card or payment details. The game keeps a count of coins and hints you bought and whether you own Remove ads, on your device (and in your cloud save, if you use it), so purchases are not lost. The Google Play Billing Library also sends Google technical logs about its own use, such as whether a request worked and connection problems, so Google can improve it and help with errors.

6a. Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To see how players get on with the levels (for example, where many players stop, so we can make new levels in time), the games send Google Analytics for Firebase short reports about play: when a level starts, is solved (with the time taken, the tools used and whether it was perfect) or is failed (with the reason) or skipped, when a tool or a continue is used, when a rewarded ad gives its reward, the tutorial steps, and when you reach certain levels for the first time (for example level 50). The SDK also records app starts, sessions, screens, app updates and Google Play purchases (the product and price), and Google's ad SDK adds when an ad is requested, is shown and gives its reward. With these reports, Google receives a random number for this installation of the game (app-instance ID), the device type and Android version, and an approximate location (country or region) worked out from your IP address. In the EU, UK and Switzerland, Google doesn't store your IP address. We do not send your name, email, advertising ID, Play Games ID or any other account ID, and we turn off Google signals and every use of these statistics for ads. Google processes this data for us, and we only see totals and charts. We keep a weekly copy of these totals (counts per game and level only, with no installation numbers and no very small counts), so that we can plan the games over time. In the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland, statistics are sent only if you agree in the consent message, and you can change this in Settings > Your privacy choices. Everywhere, you can turn statistics off in Settings > Share game statistics.

7. Internet time and connection check

To reset daily limits fairly, the game asks a Google server (www.google.com) for the current time. The game uses the same request to see whether you're online, for the offline limit (5 levels without Remove ads). Only the time and whether the server answered are read; no identifier is sent. As with any internet connection, the server sees your IP address.

8. Game-specific notes

  • Switchlight: everything above applies.
  • Peekwise: everything above applies. The pictures were made with the help of AI tools before release; the game does not send anything to an AI service.
  • Lumidart: everything above applies. Lift is a game item like hints and is stored on your device.
  • Ridelight: everything above applies. Tools (such as Undo, Crane or Extra bay) are game items like hints and are stored on your device. Expert levels you skip are stored with your level progress.
  • Test groups: if you join one of our test groups on Google Groups to try a game before release, we see the email address of your Google account. We use it only to run the test and to answer your feedback, and we don't share it (legal basis: your consent, by joining). Leave the group to be removed; when a test ends, we remove its members.

9. Why we use your data (legal bases, for players in the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Running the game, cloud save and purchases: to provide the game you asked for (contract).
  • Personalized ads: your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings > Your privacy choices.
  • Non-personalized ads, fraud prevention, keeping daily limits fair and the offline limit: our legitimate interest in funding free games and preventing abuse.
  • Game statistics: in the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland your consent, which you can withdraw in Settings > Your privacy choices; elsewhere our legitimate interest in improving the games.

10. Who receives data

We don't sell your data for money. Data goes to Google (AdMob, Google Play Billing, Google Play Games, Google Analytics for Firebase and the time check) and, for ads, to the ad technology partners Google works with (section 5). Under some US state laws, using data for personalized ads can count as "selling" or "sharing"; you can opt out (section 12). Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under its own safeguards. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

11. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you clear the app's storage or uninstall.
  • Cloud save and achievements: in your Google account until you delete them (section 12).
  • Ads, purchases and Play Games data held by Google: according to Google's retention policies.
  • Game statistics: Google keeps data linked to an installation for 2 months; after that only totals remain. We keep our weekly copy of the totals for as long as we make the games.
  • We keep no copy on servers of our own. If you email us, we keep the email for up to 12 months. If we send you a gift code, we note only your support reference number with it, never your name or email address.

12. Your choices and how to delete your data

  • Do you have to give us data? No law requires you to. Play Games, personalised ads and writing to us are all optional. If you skip Play Games, the game still works but saves only on your device, without cloud save or achievements. If you don't consent to personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads. Purchases need a Google Play account, and the ads between levels can only be turned off by buying Remove ads.
  • Change ad consent: Settings > Your privacy choices (shown where required).
  • Game statistics: in the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland, Settings > Your privacy choices. Everywhere: Settings > Share game statistics. Clearing the app's storage also gives the game a new random installation number. To have past statistics deleted: Settings > Ask us to delete my statistics (at the bottom of Settings).
  • US states: in some US states you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal data for targeted ads: Settings > Your privacy choices > "Don't sell or share my data" > Save and close (shown where it applies).
  • Delete local data: Android Settings > Apps > [game] > Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall the game.
  • Delete your cloud save in the game: Settings > Delete cloud save.
  • Delete cloud save and achievements through Google: on the web at play.google.com/games/profile > Your Data > Delete individual game data > [game] > Delete; on Android: Play Store > your profile icon > Settings > General > Play Games Profile > Privacy & Settings > Delete individual game data. You can also delete your whole Play Games profile there. (Google's help page)
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your Android settings (Privacy > Ads, wording depends on the device).
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent. Since we hold no data on servers of our own, most requests are handled through the steps above or by Google; email us and we will help. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

13. Children

Our games are made for adults and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used our games, contact us and we will help remove any data.

14. Security

All data the games send is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Our own code stores no data on servers.

15. Changes

If we change this policy, we update it on this page and change the "Last updated" date. Important changes (for example a new kind of data) are also mentioned in the game's update notes.
