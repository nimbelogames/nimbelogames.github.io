Help and support

Stuck on a level, a purchase or a setting? Quick answers below. Still stuck? Write to us.

Contact

support@nimbelogames.com

A real person on our small team reads every message. We usually reply within a few days. To help us help you, please mention:

  • the game (Switchlight or Peekwise),
  • your phone model and Android version,
  • what happened, and what you expected.

Common questions

How do I restore a purchase, like Remove ads?

Open the game, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Google account you bought it with. Restore purchases brings back Remove ads. Coins and hints stay with your progress on your phone; cloud save, when it arrives, will let you move them to a new phone.

I bought coins but didn't get them.

Close and reopen the game with an internet connection, or tap Restore purchases in Settings. If the coins still don't appear, write to us with the date of purchase and the order number from your Google Play receipt email.

How do I ask for a refund?

Use Google Play's refund request. If it's been more than 48 hours since you bought it, write to us with your Google Play order number.

Why do I see ads?

Ads help keep the games free. Ads between levels start after the first few levels and can be removed with a one-time purchase. Ads that give you a reward, such as a free hint, are always your choice.

What is Relaxed mode?

A setting in each game that turns off timers and stops levels from being lost. It's there for anyone who'd rather play without pressure.

How does the prize wheel work?

Win 5 levels in a row for a free spin, or spin after watching an optional ad, a few times a day. Prizes are coins or hints, with the odds shown in the game. The wheel can't be bought, and nothing in our games is a paid random prize.

Can I keep my progress on a new phone?

Cloud save is coming. For now, your progress is stored on your phone.

Do the games work offline?

Yes, you can play offline. Ads and purchases need an internet connection.

How do I delete my saved progress?

Your progress is stored on your phone. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes it. More in our privacy policy.

Where is the privacy policy?

Here: privacy policy. You can also open it from Settings in each game.

Can I play on iPhone?

Not yet. Our games come to Android first.
