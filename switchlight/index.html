How it plays

Each level is a 5x5 grid of lights. Tap a light and it flips, together with the lights above, below, left and right of it. Turn them all off to solve the level. It sounds simple. It isn't always.

Questions

Is Switchlight free?

Yes, it's free to play. From level 6 there's an ad between levels, and a one-time purchase removes those ads. Other ads are always your choice.

How do hints work?

A hint shows you the next light to tap. You get hints from Daily Rewards and the prize wheel. When you have none, you can use 30 coins or watch an ad for a free one.

What are coins for?

You earn coins by solving levels. You spend them on hints, extra moves or extra time. You can also buy coins in the Shop. They are game currency only and have no cash value.

What does "Remove ads" remove?

The ads between levels. Ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available, so you can still use them for a free hint or a bonus spin.

What is Relaxed mode?

A setting that turns off the timer and the move limit. No level can be lost, so you can take all the time you like. Extra-hard bonus coins aren't paid in Relaxed mode.

What is the prize wheel?

Win 5 levels in a row and you get a free spin for coins or hints. You can also spin after watching an optional ad, a few times a day. The odds of every prize are shown in the game. The wheel can't be bought.

Can I play without internet?

Yes, the puzzles play offline. Ads and Shop purchases need a connection.

I changed phones. How do I get Remove ads back?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Google account you bought it with. It brings back Remove ads. Coins and hints stay with your progress on your phone.

Something's wrong. Who do I tell?

Write to support@nimbelogames.com, or see the support page.
